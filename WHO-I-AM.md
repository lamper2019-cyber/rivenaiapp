## The basics

I'm **Sean Williams**, 25, a Black man based in **Montgomery, Alabama**.
Solo founder. I built RIVEN myself, every line of code, with Claude Code
as my pair. No no-code dependencies, no black boxes I don't understand.
If something ships under my name, I want to be able to open the file and
read it.

I currently sub at school for income while RIVEN is growing, run a
paying car-wash voice-agent build for a real client on the side, and
work on a private fantasy-series writing hub with my close friend
Elijah. RIVEN is the main thing — the rest is cash flow, craft, and
keeping creative muscles warm.

## Why I built RIVEN

Two things, stacked.

**One — my own transformation.** I lost 60+ pounds (241 → 184). Not on
a diet program, not by hating my body into compliance. I built a system
that was calm, consistent, culturally honest, and actually fit my life.
Once it worked on me, I knew the same approach would work on the women
in my world.

**Two — the women in my world.** I'm a Black man building for Black
women 35+ because the wellness industry doesn't see them as full
humans. It either ignores them, talks down to them, or sells them
shame. The women I'm building for look like my mom, my aunts, my
sisters, my friends. That's not abstract market research. That's
personal.

The brand voice — *peaceful discipline, steady wins, calm certainty,
cultural respect* — is the version of coaching I wish more Black women
had access to. Soul Food cheat-sheet, real cultural foods on the meal
log, no diet-industry hype, no fitness-bro energy. The test every
screen, every copy line, every feature passes is: **does this feel like
peaceful discipline?**

## How I think and work

- **I want to be in control.** I'd rather understand a piece of code
  fully and ship it slower than hand it to a tool I don't trust. If
  you're writing for me, comment generously and call out tradeoffs so I
  can decide.
- **I think visually.** When comparing UI ideas, give me ASCII
  wireframes labeled Option A / B / C, not prose, not JSX. I want to
  see the shape of the page.
- **I move fast and ship a lot.** When I'm locked in, the output is
  real. The flip side is I can over-extend. If I'm asking for a fifth
  feature before users have tested the third, push back.
- **Plain English over jargon.** On business stuff especially. Don't
  buzzword me.
- **Honest pushback beats yes-man energy.** If my logic is shaky, say
  so and give me the alternative. I'll respect honesty more than
  comfort.
- **Tone matters.** No hype. No "let's gooo." No toxic positivity. That
  energy is exactly what I built RIVEN to be the opposite of, and it
  doesn't land with me personally either.

This isn't a side project I'm A/B-testing for fun. RIVEN is:

- **A real, live, shipping SaaS product** — rivenmethod.com, real
  Stripe live mode, real paying customers. $50/mo, 7-day trial,
  single tier, no upsell ladder. The original 8 beta clients are
  comped for life as a thank-you.
- **My main revenue priority for the next 12 months.** The path to
  scale is software, not coaching. I'm a founder building software for

- **My proof of what I can build.** I started as someone who lost 60
  pounds. I'm becoming someone who builds the systems that help other
  people do the same. RIVEN is that transition in code.

Treat the work in this repo accordingly. Every commit goes to a real
woman's pocket who's trying to change her life. That's the standard.
